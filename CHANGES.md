# Changes

## Unreleased

Needs the matching VR room (the release after 0.2.0), whose avatar builder draws the new faces,
hair, hats, glasses, costumes and legs. The version numbers that state this (`version.php`,
`composer.json`) are raised when this is released.

- **Faces that look like you:** 12 head shapes, 21 eyes with 17 eye colours (two different ones
  too) and 5 spacings, 20 eyebrows in any hair colour, 15 noses, 16 mouths, 15 lip colours, ear
  shapes, cheeks, freckles, moles, birthmarks and face paint, age and expression lines, and
  make-up. Every choice is open to everyone; nothing is named, grouped or ordered by gender.
- **Hair:** 41 styles, from cropped and thinning to coils, braids, twists and locs, grey at the
  temples or salt and pepper, and 19 kinds of facial hair in their own colour.
- **Hats and glasses:** 33 hats (everyday, smart, faith and tradition, work and uniform,
  celebration, dress-up) and 17 kinds of glasses and sunglasses in 13 frame colours.
- **50 new costumes** made by the avatar builder itself, worn by your own avatar (your body, skin,
  face and hair): travel, work, service, health, science, school, everyday, formal, traditional
  dress, sport, and fun and fantasy. The traditional dress (yukata, hanbok, sari, qipao, kurta,
  dashiki, kilt) is a simplified drawing from general knowledge, not reviewed by people who wear
  it; corrections are welcome.
- **Easier to browse:** the Face, Hair and Extras tabs show one section at a time (for example
  Eyes, or Nose and mouth), costumes one category at a time, and long lists come under headings.
  Only the pictures in view are drawn.
- **Legs and walking:** the preview shows the whole avatar, arms that move, and buttons to see it
  walk and wave.
- **A real wave, and moves:** the wave raises the arm to the side and rocks the forearm, palm to
  the front. "Bust a move" plays a short dance (a spin, a shuffle, a disco point, a jump, the
  robot, and a hat tip when you wear a hat). In the full-length view the avatar now and then
  plays one by itself, unless your device asks for reduced motion.
- **A better-balanced face:** the eyes, nose and mouth sit lower and the chin is shorter on every
  head shape. A new "Face layout" choice (balanced, high eyes, low eyes, long nose, compact) moves
  them a little more. Avatars saved before get the new balanced layout.
- **More hats:** a pirate tricorne with a skull and crossbones and a feather, and a fez; the
  fedora and the cowboy hat were reshaped so they read at a glance.
- **Accessories:** a new Accessories tab with neckwear (ties, scarves, chains, pearls, a medal, a
  lanyard), a pin or flower on the chest, earrings, a flower behind the ear, something on each
  wrist and each ankle, in gold, silver, rose gold or black and any fabric colour. They move with
  the avatar and hide under costumes that cover them.
- **Tattoos:** up to four, each a design, a place, a size and an ink, on bare skin. The site
  setting "Tattoos on avatars" (on by default) turns them off. A second set in the style of
  traditional cultural tattoos (Māori-style kirituhi, Samoan, Hawaiian, Filipino batok, Amazigh,
  mehndi, Japanese irezumi, Celtic knotwork) is offered only when "Traditional cultural tattoos"
  is turned on (off by default); it is drawn from general knowledge and not yet checked by people
  from those cultures.
- **Hands:** a new natural hand, now the default; the cartoon, glove, robot and paw hands were
  redrawn slimmer, and every style can point and make a fist. The round and mitten hands were
  retired: avatars saved with them get natural hands.
- **The ready-made looks** were redone with the new choices, across ages, with skin-toned hands;
  the standard look has cartoon hands in the skin tone and natural hair and eye colours.
- **Removed:** the 18 Blocky characters, the two pirate models and the pirate hat model. Avatars
  saved with them keep working: the pirates become the new pirate captain and deckhand, a Blocky
  character the plain clothes in your own colours, and the pirate hat the pirate tricorne.
- **Removed: the costume models whose arms could not move** (they could not wave, dance, reach in a
  headset, walk or sit): the two suits, the twelve Mini characters, the sunflower, the cactus, the
  two aliens and the two robots. The Costume tab now offers the 50 costumes made in code and the
  four screens (laptop, computer screen, old television, desktop computer). Avatars and room
  characters saved with a removed model wear the nearest costume made in code: the suits the suit,
  the Mini characters summer clothes, the police uniform, the suit, the office outfit, the skirt,
  the tracksuit or the explorer, the aliens the astronaut, and the plants and robots the plain
  clothes in your own colours.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-27) — ALPHA

Needs VR room 0.2.0 (version 2026112700), which draws the new hands and faces in live rooms.

- **A more visual "My VR avatar" page.** The choices are on tabs with icons (Looks, Face, Hair,
  Body, Costume, Hands, Extras). Every part is a picture of the user's own avatar wearing it, and
  colours and skin tones are rows of named round swatches. Costumes can be shown one category at a
  time.
- **Easy start.** Six ready-made looks and a *Surprise me* button give a starting point to change
  from. On a first visit short tips over the preview name the next step (pick a look, change a
  part, save); the *?* button shows them again.
- **Undo and Redo**, and a **Save bar** that stays at the bottom of the window and says when there
  are unsaved changes. Leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first.
- **Hands:** a hand style (round, mittens, cartoon, gloves, robot, paws) and a hand colour.
- **Face style:** a face screen on the head, as before, or the face drawn on the skin.
- **A friendlier standard look** for people who have not designed an avatar: a drawn face, short
  hair, and a skin tone and body colour that always stay the same for that person.
- **The preview** frames what is being chosen (the head, the raised hands, or the whole figure with
  its name tag) and moves gently, unless the browser asks for reduced motion. On a phone the preview
  and the tabs stay at the top of the screen while choosing.
- Avatars saved with 0.1.x keep their look: the new choices default to what those avatars had.
- Uninstalling the plugin now also removes the avatars users saved.
